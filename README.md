## 📋 Executive Summary

We are digitizing the ancient West African financial habits of **Ajo**, **Esusu**, and **Adashe** to transition community savings from informal "Social Capital" into **"Programmable Prosperity."** By leveraging trustless escrow infrastructure (*Trustless Work on Stellar*), we eliminate the "three killers" of traditional thrift: trust friction, geographic lock-in, and lack of record-keeping.

Our platform not only provides 0% interest credit and structured savings but is evolving into a **Collab Community** where collective capital funds rentable local amenities, yielding passive dividends for every contributor.

## 💎 User Value Flow

The platform is designed to be a **"Moving Bank" in your pocket**, inspired by the legendary efficiency of *Alajọ Ṣomolu*.


```

[ 1. Initialize & Verify ] ──> [ 2. Create/Join Clique™ ] ──> [ 3. Automated Rotation ]

```

### 1. Initialize & Verify
*   **Wallet Validation:** Users connect a Stellar-active wallet. Our *Wallet Validation Gate* ensures the account is funded with XLM and has a USDC trustline enabled before any action occurs.
*   **KYC Integration:** Every member is verified via NIN/BVN, transforming traditional "social trust" into **verified accountability**.

### 2. Create or Join a "Clique™"
*   **Start a Group:** Users can create private thrift circles for colleagues, family, or trade associations.
*   **Define Milestones:** The creator sets the contribution amount (e.g., ₦50,000 monthly) and the payout rotation schedule.
*   **Invite Colleagues:** Send unique invite links to colleagues. Once they join and validate their wallets, the smart contract automatically manages the cycle.

### 3. Automated Rotation
*   **Non-Custodial Escrow:** Funds are held securely in **Soroban smart contracts**, not on our centralized servers. No collector can "vanish" with the pot.
*   **Instant Payouts:** When a milestone is met, the pot is automatically released to the assigned recipient’s wallet within minutes.

## 🚀 [COMING SOON] The Collab Community Flow

We are expanding beyond simple rotation into a decentralized investment ecosystem for community amenities.


```

[ Verified Issuance ] ──> [ Project Board State ] ──> [ Community Investment ] ──> [ Passive Dividends ]

```

### 🛠️ The Project Lifecycle

1.  **Verified Issuance:** A verified issuer—such as a local government, recognized organization, or community leader—proposes a project for a *Rentable Community Amenity*.
2.  **Project State:** The issuer creates a public project board stating:
    *   **The Asset:** (e.g., a shared cooling van for a market, solar charging stations, or a communal processing mill).
    *   **Cost & Details:** Total funding required and operational lifespan.
3.  **Community Investment:** Different community investors fund the "amenities" by contributing capital directly to the project pot.
4.  **Passive Earnings & Proportional Dividends:** Once the amenity is deployed and rented out, the revenue generated is routed back through our **Multi-Release Escrow**. Investors earn passive income automatically distributed to their wallets based strictly on their percentage share of the total contribution.
